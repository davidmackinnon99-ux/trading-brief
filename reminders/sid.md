The tramlines aren’t flat at SID entries. They’re nearly always tilted against the trade: falling into a long, rising into a short. The middle of the range is about −0.8% per bar for longs and +0.6% per bar for shorts. A “Tramline Neutral” tag would apply to only about 1% of entries. FDL was the exception, not the usual picture.
Where the entry sits:
Longs: about two-thirds close above the falling channel on the entry bar. The bounce candle breaks out of the pullback channel.
Shorts: about 80% close at or below the lower rail of the rising channel. The other 20% are still inside it, and those are the ones that did better (69% vs 61%).
BB midpoint: 99% of entries are already below it for longs and above it for shorts, typically 1.6–1.7 ATR away. That’s built into SID (RSI under 50 at a long entry), so a below/above-BB-mid tag would be true almost every time.
