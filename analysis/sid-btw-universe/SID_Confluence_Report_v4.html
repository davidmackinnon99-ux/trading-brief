01Executive Summary

This report presents a quantitative analysis of confluence factors for the SID Method — an Overbought/Oversold (OB/OS) bounce strategy using RSI extremes with MACD directional confirmation, targeting RSI-50 as its exit. The analysis covers 2,932 automated signals across a 99-ticker universe spanning 15+ years of daily data.

The overall long trade win rate of 61.3% (full history) with average profit factor of 1.98 demonstrates clear positive expectancy when regime alignment is applied. Post-2020, longs average 55.5% WR — reflecting the higher-volatility environment of recent years.

The critical finding is the primacy of the Bearish/Bullish Regime Filter: restricting long entries to periods when SMA50 > SMA200 is the most powerful single quality discriminator in the dataset. Trades taken against the regime represent Very Low Odds and are excluded from the filtered universe.

03Regime FilterPrimary Quality Gate

The Bearish/Bullish Regime Filter is the primary and most powerful quality discriminator in the dataset. Longs are only taken when SMA50 > SMA200 (bullish regime), and shorts only when SMA50 < SMA200 (bearish regime). Trades against the prevailing regime are classified as Very Low Odds and excluded.

The SMA band framework provides further granularity for entries that pass the regime filter:

Key Structural Finding
An OS entry condition inherently means price has been falling — placing it below SMA50 by definition. The Trade Band (between SMA50 and SMA200) is therefore the highest achievable quality tier for long entries, producing 65.6% WR vs 58.2% WR in the Lower Odds band.

The 20–25 Transition Zone
ADX 20–25 is the single worst-performing band. Trend strength is ambiguous — enough ADX to look like a trending market but insufficient to sustain the move required for a clean OB/OS bounce. Practical rule: prefer ADX 25–40. Treat ADX <25 with caution.

SL Distance is the Primary Stop Predictor
Stop loss distance is a stronger predictor of hard stop hits than any other single factor. Trades with SL <3% from entry have a stop hit rate exceeding 70%. Trades with SL >8% have a stop hit rate below 40%. The 2–4 bar window provides the best balance between confirmation and natural SL room.

A 34.6% WR difference between trades with and without RSI re-entry makes this the most powerful exit signal identified. When RSI crosses back into OS territory during a long trade, the bounce has failed and the position should be exited. Using a minor 2-bar RSI wobble as an exit rule is not supported — it frequently exits trades that would have succeeded.

12Confluence Framework SummaryRecommendations
Reframing SID: Trend Pullback Continuation
The data supports characterising SID not as a generic OB/OS bounce strategy but as a trend pullback continuation pattern. The strategy works best when three conditions are simultaneously present:

  1. A clear underlying trend — confirmed by SMA regime alignment (SMA50 > SMA200 for longs) and ADX ≥25
  2. A temporary exhaustion move — the counter-move that pushed RSI into the OB/OS extreme
  3. A clean bounce immediately after entry — no RSI re-entry into the OS zone during the trade

This reframing explains why Very Low Odds (SMA50 on wrong side of SMA200) underperforms — without regime alignment the RSI extreme is just chop, not exhaustion. It explains why ADX 20–25 is the worst band — insufficient trend to make the exhaustion meaningful. And it explains why clean bounces produce near-100% WR — the trend resumes without the counter-move extending.

Live Entry Checklist
1. SID signal fires (RSI extreme + MACD direction) — mandatory
2. SMA50 > SMA200 for longs / SMA50 < SMA200 for shorts — mandatory gate
3. Weekly RSI pointing in trade direction — visual mandatory check
4. Price in Trade Band (SMA50–SMA200) → preferred · Lower Odds → acceptable with confluence
5. ADX 25–40 → preferred · ADX <25 → reduce size or skip · ADX >40 → monitor closely
6. MACD histogram moderately negative (−1.0 to −0.3) → quality upgrade
7. Entry 2–4 bars after OS/OB touch → preferred · confirm SL distance ≥4%
8. Price pattern present (H&S / Double Top / Double Bottom) → further upgrade
9. MACD has crossed signal line before entry → bonus confirmation
10. Short trade → apply higher threshold · restrict to bear regime only · reduce size
11. High-ATR instrument (daily vol >3%) → reduce size · consider hard price stop

Steps 1–3 are mandatory gates. Steps 4–11 determine position size and confidence level.
